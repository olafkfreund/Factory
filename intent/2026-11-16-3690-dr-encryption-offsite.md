# Intent: decide R-007, encrypt off-site copies, wire scoped Job S3

Issues: Factory#3690 (R-007 HA), Factory#3691 (MinIO backup, snapshot
encryption), Factory#3681 (off-cluster copy destination), Factory#314
(encryption at rest, offline key backup), AIFactory#2023 (Job S3 credentials).

## Problem

Five open decisions block the data-protection issues, and the owner delegated
them on 2026-11-16 ("use the recommendations"):

- R-007: Postgres and MinIO are single instances on one physical host. The
  owner has to accept the risk or provide a second failure domain.
- The off-site copies (`apps/backups/offsite`, `apps/backups/minio-offsite`)
  are written but not deployed, and they upload plaintext. Whether the copies
  are encrypted before they leave the cluster is undecided.
- The destination for the copies is undecided.
- #314: the offline backup of the age identity that decrypts the MinIO KEK is
  unconfirmed.
- AIFactory#2023: AIFactory can give each Job scoped STS credentials, but the
  flag and the STS Secret refs are not in the Deployment, and the MinIO hook
  only creates the STS user once a human adds the keys to `minio-creds`.

## Proposed outcome

- R-007 is recorded as accepted (AR-003), decided under the owner's chat
  delegation of 2026-11-16 ("Use the recommendations to solve all issues",
  recorded in each issue as "Decision (owner delegation 2026-11-16)"), with a
  review date, compensating controls and revisit triggers. The register says
  "Decided (delegated)", not signed: the Security Owner's signature line stays
  open and is an owner action.
- The off-site jobs can encrypt every upload with age to a public recipient
  the owner supplies (private key offline). Off by default; with no recipient
  set they behave exactly as today.
- The destination is PENDING the owner's pick. Constraint: a private, versioned
  S3-compatible bucket outside the house (recommended in the #3681 thread:
  Backblaze B2 or Hetzner, EU). MinIO on p510 leaves the host, not the site,
  and is a fallback only. Nothing is deployed until the owner seeds the Secret.
- The aifactory Deployment carries `AIFACTORY_SCOPED_JOB_S3=false` and optional
  `S3_STS_*` refs, so enabling is one Secret edit and one flag flip.
- Each issue gets a decision comment and the exact owner steps. None of the
  issues is closed by this work except where noted in the spec: #3681,
  #3691, #314 and AIFactory#2023 stay open for the owner steps (destination
  Secret, restore from the off-site copy, full-path RTO, offline identity
  check, the first live scoped build and spec Job).

## Affected users and systems

Security Owner (signature), the operator (secrets), `docs/compliance/*` in
Factory, `apps/backups/*`, `apps/aifactory`, `docs/secrets-management.md` in
factory-gitops (Argo CD syncs main).

## Constraints

- No secret value is created, read or printed. Minting the age key pair, the
  STS keys and the destination credentials is the owner's.
- Nothing may change live behaviour until the owner acts: no new CronJob is
  referenced by a synced app, the flag stays false.
- No live-cluster change by the agent.
- Off-site jobs never delete or overwrite at the destination.

## Open questions

Taken under delegation, recorded in the spec: accept AR-003; age to an offline
key; STS keys cannot be generated by the existing hook, so the wiring is
default-off. Still the owner's: the destination provider and credentials, the
age key pair, the STS keys, the AR-003 signature, the offline identity check.
