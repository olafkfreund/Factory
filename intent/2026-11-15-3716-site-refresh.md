# Intent: refresh the five Factory GitHub Pages sites

## Problem

The public sites show the suite as it was in August. Visitors, and anyone
sent a link from a post or a demo, see features and versions that are three
months old.

| Site | Source | Newest post | Homepage last changed | Shipped since 2026-08-10 |
| ---- | ------ | ----------- | --------------------- | ------------------------ |
| factory.freundcloud.com | Factory `main:/docs` (Jekyll) | 2026-08-10 | 2026-07-30 | 166 merged PRs; roadmap last updated 2026-10-24 |
| aifactory.freundcloud.com | AIFactory `docs/` (Docusaurus → `gh-pages`) | 2026-08-10 | 2026-06-21 | 35 releases, v3.6.74 → v3.10.16 |
| pfactory.freundcloud.com | PFactory `main:/docs` (Jekyll) | 2026-08-10 | 2026-09-03 | 26 releases, v0.6.17 → v0.6.42; roadmap from June |
| tfactory.freundcloud.com | TFactory `main:/docs` (Jekyll) | 2026-08-10 | 2026-08-05 | 24 releases, v0.9.26 → v0.9.49 |
| cfactory.freundcloud.com | CFactory `main:/docs` (Jekyll) | 2026-08-10 | 2026-07-26 | 116 merged PRs; roadmap from June |

Commits that touch `docs/` since August come from the intent/spec/plan
convention and compliance pages. They did not change the public story.

## Proposed outcome

- Each site's homepage describes what the product does today: headline
  features, current version and status, and links that work.
- Each site has one new post covering what shipped from 2026-08-10 to
  2026-11-15. Every claim in it links to a release, PR or RFC.
- Roadmap pages (Factory, PFactory, CFactory; TFactory `progress.md`) say what
  shipped and what is next as of November 2026.
- factory.freundcloud.com, the umbrella site, sums up the whole suite and
  links to all four product sites.
- All five sites are live with the new content, checked in a browser.

## Affected users and systems

- Repositories: Factory, AIFactory, PFactory, TFactory, CFactory (`docs/` only).
- Readers of the five sites. Posts and demos that link to them.
- No services, clusters or hosts are touched.

## Constraints

- Docs only: no code, workflow or config changes outside `docs/`.
- AIFactory, PFactory, TFactory and CFactory default to `dev`. Their sites
  deploy only from `main` (via a release or a dev→main merge). Factory deploys
  straight from `main`.
- Every claim must hold up against a release note, a merged PR or an RFC
  status. No roadmap items described as shipped. This follows the
  2026-08-10 "only claim it when you can prove it" line.
- Keep each site's existing layout, theme and URLs. No redesign.
- No customer names, private hosts or secrets in public pages.

## Open questions

1. **Scope:** homepage, new post and roadmap on all five sites (proposed), or
   a smaller cut (for example only a post per site)?
2. **Going live:** for the four `dev`-default repos, should the docs ride the
   next normal release, or get a docs-only dev→main promotion now?
3. **Screenshots:** reuse the existing images (proposed), or capture new
   portal screenshots from the k3d cluster on p620?
4. **MCP-AgentGateway** (v0.8.1, 10 releases, no site): mention it on
   factory.freundcloud.com, or leave it out?
