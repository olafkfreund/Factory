# Intent: R-007 HA options for Postgres and MinIO on the single-node cluster

## Problem

Risk R-007 (Postgres and MinIO are single instances on node-local storage) is
Open at score 12, target 6. SoA A.8.14 is Planned, which holds SOC 2 CC9.1 and
A1.2 at Gap. The owner has to choose between "build a second failure domain"
and "accept the risk with a review date" (as AR-001, AR-002 and R-014 did).

The evidence for the choice exists but is spread out: measured restore and RPO
in `docs/compliance/dr-runbook.md`, the single-physical-host finding there,
versioning (a control, not a backup) and the not-yet-deployed off-site backup
(Factory#3681, Factory#3691). What is missing is one owner-facing comparison:
which HA options are achievable on a one-host k3d cluster, which are not, what
each costs, and what RPO/RTO each leaves. Without it the decision has no
usable input, and the risk-acceptance entry has no draft to sign.

## Proposed outcome

- One document the owner can decide from: each option, what it buys, what it
  cannot buy on one physical host, cost, RPO/RTO, and the exact commands.
- RPO/RTO stated from the existing backups and the restore drill, with the
  unmeasured parts named.
- A ready-to-sign risk-acceptance entry text (unsigned; the agent does not sign).
- R-007 and the DR runbook's outstanding list link to that document.
- No live-cluster change; no change to Postgres/MinIO replicas or storage.

## Affected users and systems

Compliance owner / Security Owner (decision). `docs/compliance/` in Factory
only. Read-only evidence from `factory-gitops/apps/{postgres,minio,backups}`.

## Constraints

- No live change; no kubectl apply/delete. Replica or storage changes are the
  owner's decision (they need hardware or cost the agent cannot supply).
- PodDisruptionBudgets stay out: `factory-gitops/apps/README.md` records a
  measured decision (Factory#550) that a PDB over one replica blocks node drain.
  Re-adding them would contradict it.
- Agent output must not claim sign-off for a risk acceptance.

## Open questions

- Owner: build a second failure domain, or sign the acceptance? (Stays open;
  the deliverable gives both paths.)
