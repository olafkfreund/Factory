# Intent: R-007 HA options for Postgres and MinIO on the single-node cluster

## Problem

Risk R-007 (Postgres and MinIO are single instances on node-local storage) is
Open at score 12, target 6. SoA A.8.14 is Planned, which holds SOC 2 CC9.1 and
A1.2 at Gap. The owner has not decided between "build a second failure domain"
and "accept the risk with a review date". Nothing in the repo says what HA is
actually achievable on this cluster, or what RPO/RTO the existing backups and
restore drill really give. `docs/compliance/dr-runbook.md` is also stale: it
says MinIO is not protected, but versioning and lifecycle rules have since
landed in factory-gitops (`apps/minio`, `apps/backups`).

## Proposed outcome

- One document the owner can decide from: each HA option, what it buys, what it
  cannot buy on one physical host, cost, and the exact commands to apply it.
- RPO/RTO stated from the existing backups and the 2026-08-10 restore drill,
  with the unmeasured parts named.
- The DR runbook corrected to match what is deployed.
- A ready-to-sign risk-acceptance entry text for the owner (not signed by the
  agent).
- No live-cluster change, no change to Postgres/MinIO replicas or storage.

## Affected users and systems

Compliance owner / Security Owner (decision). `docs/compliance/` in Factory
only. Read-only evidence from `factory-gitops/apps/{postgres,minio,backups}`.

## Constraints

- No live change; no kubectl apply/delete. Replica or storage changes are the
  owner's decision (they need hardware or cost the agent cannot supply).
- PodDisruptionBudgets stay out: `factory-gitops/apps/README.md` records a
  measured decision (Factory#550) that a PDB over one replica blocks node drain.
  Re-adding them would contradict it.
- Agent output must not claim sign-off for a risk acceptance.

## Open questions

- Owner: build a second failure domain, or sign the acceptance? (Stays open;
  the deliverable gives both paths.)
