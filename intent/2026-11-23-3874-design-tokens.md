# Intent: one design system for the five Factory portals

## Problem

The 2026-11-23 UI test (Factory#3873) found two visual languages across the
portals, muted text below WCAG AA on cards and on the light background,
375 sub-12px text sizes, and drifted theme tokens in each portal. CFactory
and RFactory do not share the theme at all.

## Proposed outcome

One hub-owned token set for dark and light, with a per-portal accent,
consumed by all five portals:
- the three Tailwind portals through a bridge that keeps the shadcn
  utility names;
- CFactory and RFactory through plain-CSS aliases.

Gates keep it true: drift, no text below 12px, and contrast of every token
pair.

## Affected users and systems

Factory `shared/factory-ui`, `scripts/`, CI; then every portal (C2).

## Constraints

- Owner-approved design (plan C0): Gruvbox hues as an OKLCH role ramp;
  every text and accent pair ≥ 4.5:1 on every surface; `border-strong`
  ≥ 3:1.
- Hub first; portals adopt one at a time, TFactory first with an owner
  side-by-side check.
- No change to portal behaviour in this step.

## Open questions

None. The decisions are in the approved #3873 plan.
