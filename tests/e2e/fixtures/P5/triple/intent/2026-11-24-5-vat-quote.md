# Intent: vatcalc UK invoice VAT and total quote calculator

## Problem

`vatcalc.calc.compute_quote` is an unimplemented stub that raises `NotImplementedError`, so callers cannot compute net, VAT, and gross total amounts for invoice line items.

## Proposed outcome

`vatcalc.calc.compute_quote` calculates a `Quote(net, vat, total)` using fixed-point `Decimal` arithmetic at two decimal places, enforces non-negative net amounts and VAT rates, and satisfies the invoice total calculation rules and worked examples.

## Affected users and systems

- `vatcalc/calc.py` and `tests/test_vatcalc.py` in the `vatcalc` package.

## Constraints

- Use the Python standard-library `decimal.Decimal` module only; do not use binary floating-point (`float`) or third-party dependencies.
- All currency outputs (`net`, `vat`, `total`) must be exact `decimal.Decimal` instances quantized to `Decimal("0.01")`.
